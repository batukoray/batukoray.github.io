People

I place exceptional importance on people and my relationships with them. I believe that everything we do is ultimately for people, so I care deeply about people skills, personal care, and understanding the wants and needs of those around me. I believe this fills an important space within us. The outside world completes us, and some of our deepest fulfillment comes from our relationships.

The Big Steppers

I believe life is an incredible thing that can be thought of as a multivariate function. Ultimately, you want to find the maxima of that function, which is a nonlinear combination of variables such as happiness, satisfaction, peacefulness, pride, and many others. It is obviously not a linear optimization problem. Instead, you take the gradient vector of the function and move in its direction through small but concrete steps. These steps should also reflect long-term thinking, rather than chasing a quick gain in the short term. Ask yourself: What will benefit me most in the long term? What can I do right now that I will still feel proud of years from now? What can I do to make myself happy? Then, take concrete steps in that direction.
